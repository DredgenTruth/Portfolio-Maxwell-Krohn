Abstract

The research done here was on the rations which were given to British soldiers on the front lines. Both of my grandfathers served in some branch of the United States armed forces, and they have both told me about the poor quality of rations and how they can affect performance. I wanted to learn more about the quality of rations during the first world war, and illustrate to an audience how the effects of poor rations could stack up over time (in a dramatized fashion, admittedly).
